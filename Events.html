Marriage Conference

 Our Marriage Conference is a time and place to be able to understand not only why marriage is beautiful in the eyes of the Lord, but also how to navigate its many challenges. In this conference, many topics will be discussed to help us understand as to what a fruitful marriage looks like in the Christian faith. Please contact Abigail Meyer at +1 409 443 4143 for more information

Mission

 The annual Calvary Church mission trip is a perfect way to begin missionary work. This opportunity will provide you a way to begin a closer relationship with God by helping others. Please contact Abigail Meyer at +1 409 443 4143 for more information or to sign up.

Marriage Retreat

 Here at Calvary Church, we understand that marriage is lots of work, which is why we offer a marriage retreat designed as a God-focused getaway. Like our mission trip, it is held in the Dominican Republic, which allows for the retreat to not only but fun but also to continue to keep our marriages focused on Christ. Please contact Abigail Meyer at +1 409 443 4143 for more information or to sign up.

Strong: Woman's Conference

 Strong: Woman's Conference is a conference specifically focused on the struggles that Christian women face while operating within the world. It goes over the issues of being a Christian woman in the modern day and how to solve them, while most importantly focusing on how our relationship with Christ should work as well. Please contact Abigail Meyer at +1 409 443 4143 for more information.
